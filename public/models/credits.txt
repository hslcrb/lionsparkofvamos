Stadium and district reconstructed procedurally in Blender 4.2 from the supplied aerial, plan and night facade references; unseen details are interpretive.
Build pipeline: assets/blender/lionspark_v4_build.py (standalone, no external add-ons).
Original facade sign artwork: supplied 대구 삼성 라이온즈 파크.svg.
Photographic sky: Kloofendal 38d Partly Cloudy Pure Sky by Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_38d_partly_cloudy_puresky
Concrete paving: Concrete Pavement 03 by Charlotte Baglioni, Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_pavement_03
Procedural grass, clay, asphalt, paving and roof textures: generated in Blender with numpy by the build script.
Fonts: Noto Sans KR (Adobe, SIL Open Font License 1.1), Barlow Condensed (Jeremy Tribby, SIL Open Font License 1.1). See assets/blender/fonts/OFL-*.txt.
Celestial night atlas: original procedural artwork generated in Blender MCP; statistical stars, not a star catalogue. Source: assets/blender/celestial_sky_texture.py.
Galactic pole orientation reference: Astropy Galactic frame. https://github.com/astropy/astropy/blob/main/astropy/coordinates/builtin_frames/galactic.py
Atmospheric scattering: Three.js Sky / Preetham model, MIT. https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Sky.html
Solar visual ephemeris: Meeus-style equations documented by NOAA. https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/calcdetails.html
